Author Website

A home you own

Social platforms change their rules constantly. Your website and your mailing list are the only reader relationships you actually control.

Get a quote Ask a question

What this is

An author website does three jobs: it makes you findable, it sells your books, and it captures email addresses so you are not renting your audience from a platform. Everything else is decoration.

What’s included

  • Custom design in your book’s visual language, not a stock template
  • Books, about, blog, events and contact pages
  • Mailing list capture wired to your email provider
  • Buy links routed to every retailer that carries you
  • Mobile-first build, fast loading, accessible markup
  • Search fundamentals and analytics configured at launch
  • A CMS you can update yourself, plus a walkthrough

How it runs

01Plan

Sitemap, goals and the look you want.

02Design

Homepage design first, for approval, then the inner pages.

03Build

Developed, populated with your real content, tested on real devices.

04Launch

Domain, analytics, list integration and a handover session.

Why it matters

Your list is the asset. Everything else is a channel you are borrowing.
